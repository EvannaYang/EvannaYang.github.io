Context

Gabriel AI helps businesses create personalized voice communication at scale. Campaign setup and performance tracking needed a clearer, more connected experience.

Solution

I designed the application experience around the full campaign journey: Smart Sequencing, AI-assisted script writing, voice selection, launch controls, and performance tracking.

The final work also includes the dashboard, analytics, subscription flow, interactive onboarding, and product landing page.

Design System

Established a cohesive design system using Poppins to ensure the platform could scale without losing visual integrity.
